Signal Engine listens to public Reddit feeds, ranks the problems that keep coming up, and writes a one-page morning brief with the audience's own words. Local-first. Read-only. Built to be the opposite of the AI marketing-slop pipeline.

What it actually does

  1. Listens. Polls public subreddit RSS feeds on a 30-minute cron, paced politely, with a circuit breaker if Reddit starts blocking your IP.
  2. Indexes. Stores every post and comment in local SQLite with full-text search. No cloud, no API keys required to read.
  3. Mines. Clusters recurring pain points using deterministic stats (no LLM required). With an Anthropic key, enriches with quoted language and intent scoring, capped at a hard monthly budget.
  4. Reports. Generates a daily digest at 08:00 with the day's rising pains, new phrases that didn't exist two weeks ago, the queue of actively-asking threads, and engine health.

Why the agent never posts

Most "AI finds me customers on Reddit" tools work by mass-drafting replies that read like help but funnel traffic. Reddit banned that pattern in 2026 — and the operators that ran it lost their accounts.

Signal Engine has no login, no draft-for-paste queue, no astroturfing path. The agent reads, mines, and writes to your dashboard. Distribution happens through channels you own: your site, your newsletter, your own transparent participation in the communities you serve.

This is a feature, not a limitation. It's why the accounts that do participate on Reddit survive.

Run it

pip install -e ".[dev]"
signal-engine add-subreddit smallbusiness
signal-engine fetch
signal-engine serve         # dashboard at http://localhost:7788

Cron entries for unattended collection live in docs/crontab.txt. Total monthly cost with the LLM off: zero. With the LLM on: under $25.
