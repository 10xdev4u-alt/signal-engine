How it works

Four steps. One cron. One page in your browser by 8am.

  1. 01

    Listens

    Polls public subreddit RSS feeds on a 30-minute cron, paced politely at 45 seconds between requests, with a circuit breaker if Reddit starts blocking your IP.

  2. 02

    Indexes

    Stores every post and comment in local SQLite with full-text search. No cloud, no API keys, no accounts to manage.

  3. 03

    Mines

    Clusters recurring pain points with deterministic stats (no LLM required). With an Anthropic key, enriches with quoted language and intent scoring, capped at a hard monthly budget.

  4. 04

    Reports

    Generates a daily digest at 08:00 with the day's rising pains, new phrases that didn't exist two weeks ago, the queue of actively-asking threads, and engine health.

Why read-only

The agent never posts. That's the whole point.

Most "AI finds me customers on Reddit" tools work by mass-drafting replies that read like help but funnel traffic. Reddit banned that pattern in 2026 — and the operators that ran it lost their accounts.

Signal Engine has no login, no draft-for-paste queue, no astroturfing path. The agent reads, mines, and writes to your dashboard. Distribution happens through channels you own: your site, your newsletter, your own transparent participation in the communities you serve.

This is a feature, not a limitation. It's why the accounts that do participate on Reddit survive.

Run it

Five commands to a live engine.

git clone https://github.com/10xdev4u-alt/signal-engine
cd signal-engine
python -m venv .venv && . .venv/bin/activate
pip install -e ".[dev]"

signal-engine add-subreddit smallbusiness
signal-engine fetch          # one polite pull
signal-engine serve          # dashboard at http://localhost:7788

Cron entries for unattended collection live in docs/crontab.txt. Monthly cost with the LLM off: zero. With the LLM on: under $25. Hosted on your own machine, your own data, your own call.
